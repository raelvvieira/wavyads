## Brand idea

WAVY is a unified platform for acquisition, data, sales operation, and intelligence. The interface must communicate precision, calm, technological fluency, and commercial confidence.

Core statement: **Performance becomes intelligence.**

The interface should feel like a lightweight intelligent instrument: continuous surfaces, floating contextual controls, clean data, restrained atmospheric depth, and smooth state transitions.

## Visual formula

- 60% functional minimalism
- 20% floating islands and contextual surfaces
- 10% atmosphere, lighting, and depth
- 10% expressive WAVY brand energy

Reinterpret rather than copy: floating app shell, premium white clarity, layered-charcoal dark mode, selective frosted glass, hierarchical radii, orange-red ambient current, physical segmented controls, and spatially continuous motion.

## Product principles

### Clarity before spectacle

Every effect must improve hierarchy, orientation, feedback, or brand recognition.

### One ecosystem, contextual density

Use one global shell. Adjust density by workflow:

- Dashboard: medium density, executive summaries, charts, filter islands.
- CRM: higher density, conversation and pipeline efficiency, solid reading surfaces.
- Studio/AI: lower density around canvas or conversation, contextual floating tools.

### Solid core, glass edge

Use solid surfaces for data consumption. Use glass for navigational and contextual layers.

### White means clarity

Light mode is genuinely white. Neutral gray separates levels. Avoid cream/beige canvas.

### Charcoal means depth

Dark mode uses `#111113` and nearby charcoals. Avoid a flat sea of pure black.

### Orange means energy

Orange-red indicates primary action, active connection, or brand intelligence. It cannot also mean every warning, chart series, selection, and decoration.

## Material hierarchy

- **Level 0 — Canvas:** global backdrop; optional subtle glow away from dense content.
- **Level 1 — Functional:** tables, forms, messages, data cards, charts; mostly opaque.
- **Level 2 — Island:** navigation, filters, contextual tools; moderate transparency and blur.
- **Level 3 — Overlay:** modal, command palette, detail panel, notifications; stronger elevation and focus management.

## Fluid Current signature

- Slide active indicators between navigation items.
- Morph chart state between time ranges.
- Illuminate related funnel stages in sequence.
- Originate contextual panels from their trigger.
- Use a restrained orange-red current while AI processes.
- Preserve icon positions when navigation expands.
- Highlight ad → lead → conversation → sale relationships.

Avoid liquid simulation, exaggerated elasticity, neon trails, cyberpunk effects, and distracting loops.

## Themes

### Light

White main surface; soft neutral canvas only for separation; crisp dark text; thin borders; delicate shadows; glass on floating tools; gradient on CTA and intelligence moments.

### Dark

Layered charcoals; warm glow at 10–18% visual intensity; readable muted text; smoked glass navigation; thin light borders and inset highlights.

## Anti-patterns

- Glass on every card.
- Gradients behind tables or long text.
- Orange on every series.
- One radius for every component.
- Excessive nested cards.
- Floating controls without spatial logic.
- Gray-on-gray low contrast.
- Heavy shadows everywhere.
- Motion that delays common operations.
- Landing-page density inside operational software.
- Generic neon, 3D blobs, or copied reference identities.
